# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Knowledge Graph RAG Assistant respectful, constructive, and welcoming for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Give feedback that is specific, technical, and actionable.
- Distinguish verified system behavior from assumptions and model-generated claims.
- Respect the ownership and privacy of datasets, credentials, and research artifacts.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, restricted datasets, or proprietary client material.
- Misrepresenting fixture responses, experiments, or benchmarks as live or independently verified results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants whose behavior harms the project community.
